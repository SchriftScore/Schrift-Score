The Genesis of the Schrift Score

Formula Derivation

ALL STATS VIA BASKETBALL REFERENCE • 10-Year Rolling Empirical Model

The Schrift Score is derived from a 10-year rolling statistical model created by Zachary Schriftman. Every multiplier is scientifically grounded using this 3-step formula:

Step 1: 10-Year Aggregation Sum the top 10 qualified player totals for each category over the last 10 seasons.
Step 2: Baseline Division Divide that combined 10-year aggregate number by 100 to yield the individual elite baseline.
Step 3: Inversion Calculation Divide 10 by that baseline number to compute the category's active formula multiplier.
Formula 2026–2027:
Schrift Score = (0.35 × PPG) + (0.83 × RPG) + (1.14 × APG) + (1.80 × SPG) + (1.98 × BPG) + (0.04 × GP)
Formula 2025–2026:
Schrift Score = (0.36 × PPG) + (0.84 × RPG) + (1.18 × APG) + (1.80 × SPG) + (1.99 × BPG) + (0.04 × GP)
